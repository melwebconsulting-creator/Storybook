About the Platform 关于本站

Welcome to English Adventure

"English Adventure helps Chinese-speaking students improve their English through interesting stories, vocabulary practice, interactive quizzes, and fun learning challenges."

“英语大冒险致力于帮助中文学生通过有趣的原创故事、词汇练习、互动测试和趣味学习挑战，轻松提高英语阅读与理解能力。”

Why Learning Through Stories Works 故事学习法的魅力

1. Contextual Vocabulary (语境记忆)

Words are not learned in isolation. Reading them inside genuine story plots reinforces semantic meaning and collocations naturally.

2. Bilingual Scaffold (双语脚手架)

Toggle Simplified Chinese translations at any time whenever you encounter complex sentence structures or idioms.

3. Audio Pronunciation (原声发音)

Listen to accurate native English pronunciation using browser speech synthesis directly for every vocabulary card.

4. Immediate Feedback (即时反馈测试)

Reinforce retention with 5 varied question types per story and clear bilingual explanations for every answer.
